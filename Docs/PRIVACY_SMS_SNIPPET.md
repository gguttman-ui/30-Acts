## Text Messaging (SMS)

We use text messages in two ways:

**Verification.** When you sign in, we send a one-time 6-digit code to confirm your
phone number. This is required to use your account.

**Reminders (optional).** If you turn on **Daily Reminder** in the app, we send you
automated reminder texts to log your act of kindness — up to two per day, at the
times you choose. You are asked to consent to these separately, and consent is not
a condition of using the app.

Message frequency varies. **Message and data rates may apply.** You can opt out of
reminders at any time by replying **STOP** to any reminder text, or by turning the
Daily Reminder off in the app. Reply **HELP** for help. Opting out of reminders
does not affect the verification codes needed to sign in.

**We do not share or sell your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in status to any
third party or affiliate for their marketing purposes.** Phone numbers are used
only to operate the 30 Acts of Kindness service.

Messaging is sent via our provider (Twilio) solely to deliver these messages on
our behalf.
