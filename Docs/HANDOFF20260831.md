## What the share row does now

```
Spread the kindness — invite someone to join!
              Share Social Media

    (TT)      (IG)      (X)      (FB)
   TikTok  Instagram    X     Facebook

  [ 💬 Text ] [ 📧 Email ] [ ↗️ More ]
```

**Gary confirmed all four platforms working on his iPhone today.** They were not
working this morning.

| | Opens | Arrives | You do |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | direct | picture, via Photos | paste the caption |
| Instagram | direct | picture | paste the caption |
| X | direct | caption, already typed | long-press → **Paste** the picture |
| Facebook | direct | picture, attached | paste the caption |
| Text | share sheet | card | — |
| Email | direct | card renders in the body | — |
| More | share sheet | card + caption | — |

**Each platform uses a different route on purpose.** iOS lets an app reach
another app two ways: a *URL* (carries text, never a file) and a *share
extension* (carries the file, but iOS alone decides where that app sits in the
sheet). No single route works for all four. `src/components/ShareButtons.js`
records which one each platform uses and which approaches failed on a real
device — read it before "simplifying" anything.

### X: why it looks different from the rest

X used to open the iOS share sheet, which carries the picture and caption
together and is genuinely the best result of any platform. It was abandoned
because **iOS alone orders the apps in that sheet** — on Gary's phone X sat
behind AirDrop, Messages, Mail and Facebook, reachable only by swiping the app
row. Apple exposes no way to pin an app or open a named share extension, so that
swipe could not be removed, and most people would never make it.

X now opens its composer directly with the caption already typed. The picture
cannot come through a URL, so it travels two other ways at once: onto the
clipboard, and into Photos.

The clipboard holds one thing. For the other three platforms it carries the
caption, because none of them accept prefilled text. X is the only platform that
takes prefilled text through its intent — which frees the clipboard to carry the
picture instead. **That is why pasting a picture into X works now and did not
before: the caption used to be sitting on the clipboard.**

## What every share now says

```
My act of kindness for Day 12:
"Left a thank-you note for the mail carrier"
One kind act a day for 30 days in a row.

Join me and help make the world a kinder place. Scan the code in the picture,
or tap the link:
https://alrpa.app.link/...

#30ActsOfKindness
```

It used to open "I'm doing 30 Acts of Kindness" and never say what the act was.
A reader could not tell what they were looking at.

- **Text and email** get the same shape plus the written story.
- **The certificate** says "I completed all 30 Acts of Kindness" and names no
  single act, because it is not about one.
- **A story-only day leads with the story.** Writing a story without picking an
  act leaves the title as the app's placeholder, "My Story". A first attempt at
  this shipped `Day 79: "My Story"` to a live X post, which said nothing. The
  placeholder list is in `isRealActTitle` in `src/lib/shareMessage.js`.

The act leads rather than following the challenge line for a concrete reason: X
caps a post at 280 characters and counts every link as 23. The caption is built
to that cap so all four platforms get identical words. Leading with the act buys
about 40 characters, which fits **all 152 acts** in `acts_of_kindness.csv` whole
— verified against the file. Anything longer someone types themselves is trimmed
at a word boundary.

## Also fixed today

**The act picker lost what you typed.** Reported by Ghenno. On the picker, type
a phrase that matches nothing, tap **+ Create a New Act**, and your words used to
vanish — you had to retype them and tap Save My Act.

The picker was doing its job and passing the text back. My Story ignored it:
React Navigation reuses a screen already on the stack, so `route.params` updates
but `useState` never re-runs. Someone had hit this before and patched the
*picked act* half of it; the *typed text* half was never wired up.

Rules and 15 tests in `src/lib/storySeed.js`. The rule: a suggestion may fill an
empty box or replace a suggestion we put there ourselves, but it **never**
overwrites something you wrote.

**Sentry replays.** `replaysSessionSampleRate` went from `0.1` to `0`. At 0.1
Sentry recorded a tenth of *every* session, error or not — 40 of the plan's 50
monthly replays went on recordings of the app working correctly, while the error
count sat at zero. `replaysOnErrorSampleRate` stays at `1`, so every session that
actually throws is still captured in full. `sendDefaultPii` was reviewed and
deliberately left `true`.

**PayPal donation link.** Now the Giving Fund charity page,
`paypal.com/us/fundraiser/charity/5977398`, which takes **no transaction fee**.
Changed on the website first, tested, then moved into the app. Both confirmed
working.

The website's QR image had to be regenerated — it still encoded the old
fee-charging URL, so scanning it would have quietly undone the change.
`__tests__/donations.test.js` pins the URL: the two links look equally plausible
in a diff and behave identically when tapped, and the only visible difference is
on a statement weeks later.

## Things that are correct, not bugs

- **Instagram gives you the picture and no caption.** Instagram has no way to
  receive text from another app. Paste it.
- **TikTok's "Add a catchy title" stays empty.** Same reason.
- **Facebook's caption must be pasted.** Facebook refuses prefilled text from
  third-party apps as a matter of policy.
- **X asks you to paste the picture.** A URL cannot carry a file, and X's
  composer is opened by URL. The picture is on your clipboard and in your
  Photos, so either route works.
- **Photo and video acts share the branded card, not your own photo.** The card
  carries the QR, so it wins.
- **PayPal opens with an empty amount box.** The Giving Fund URL accepts no
  amount parameter. On the bracelet screen the app copies `6.95` to your
  clipboard and says so. The fixes for this (PayPal.me, hosted button) both
  reintroduce the transaction fee — see backlog item 6.
- **Facebook cannot be tested in Expo Go** — the SDK is not linked there. Use
  the preview build.

## Notes for whoever picks up the code

- `src/components/ShareButtons.js` is the only share UI. Its header comment
  records each platform's route **and which approaches failed on a real
  device**. Read it before collapsing four buttons into one.
- `shareToX` is the most-rewritten function in the app — six routes have been
  tried. The share sheet is the only one that carries the picture, and it was
  still abandoned, because iOS buries X in the sheet and Apple gives no way to
  un-bury it. `src/lib/xIntent.js` holds the URL building and the alert wording,
  both tested.
- `src/lib/withTimeout.js` bounds every call into native share UI. A share
  handler sets `sharing = true` and clears it in a `finally`; an await that
  never settles skips that `finally` and leaves every share button on the screen
  dead to taps, with nothing on screen to explain it. It has happened twice.
  Never add a bare `await RNShare.open(...)` — the test suite will fail you.
- `StoryCard.js` renders at `top: 0, left: 0` with 1px `overflow: hidden`. Fully
  off-screen views return blank captures on iOS. It looks like dead styling. It
  is not.
- The pure, tested modules are where the rules live: `shareMessage.js` (caption
  wording and X's length cap), `xIntent.js` (X URLs, alert text),
  `storySeed.js` (what fills the story box), `shareCard.js` (hosted card for
  email), `donations.test.js` (the PayPal URL).
- `npm test` — **12 suites, 199 tests**, green as of September 1. Run before every
  push; a build passing is not the same as the suite passing.
- `eas update` exports internally. Running `npx expo export` first leaves a
  `dist/` folder that can lock and break the update with `EBUSY`. Delete `dist`
  if that happens.
