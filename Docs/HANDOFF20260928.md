## Lessons from 28 September

- **Check for personal details before a profile goes public.** Instagram took a home address and X showed a personal birth date; both were removed.
- **Copy text between apps instead of retyping it.** iPhone autocorrect turned "501(c)(3)" into "501 (c) (3)".
- **TikTok allows one account per phone number, and one name change per 7 days.** Pick the username carefully; it can change only once every 30 days.
- **Sign-up fraud filters are picky.** When a web sign-up is blocked, the phone app over cellular usually works.
