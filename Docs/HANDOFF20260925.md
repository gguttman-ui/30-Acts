## Lessons from today worth keeping

- **Legal name must match the Articles exactly.** The AG portal rejects any document that uses a different spelling. The org's legal name is `30 ACTS OF KINDNESS, NFP`, not "30ActsofKindness NFP".
- **Read the whole question.** CO-1 Q2 asks whether you solicit *or are located in* Illinois, so a located-here charity answers Yes.
- **Figures must agree across forms.** A $0 contribution beside $36 cash is what triggered the AG's question.
