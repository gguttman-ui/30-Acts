## Lessons from 26-27 September

- **A link that works in a personal text is not proof it works in a business text.** Carriers filter business texts from toll-free numbers harder. Only a real send through Twilio proves delivery.
- **Credit is keyed on a phone number, not an account**, so referral credit can wait for someone who hasn't signed up yet.
- **Read a function on the target database before replacing it.** A hash mismatch can be copy-paste spacing; diff the text itself.
- **Check that published contact addresses actually exist.** privacy@ and support@ were in the policy but bounced until 27 Sep.
- **TestFlight invitations expire unnoticed.** Check the tester status ("Invited" versus "Installed") before assuming someone has the build.
