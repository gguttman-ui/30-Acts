 67% of Americans say people are less kind than 20 years ago, yet 92% believe kindness matters. This gap represents a $12B market opportunity. People want to be kinder but lack structure, accountability, and recognition. Loneliness is epidemic (58% feel no one knows them well), social fragmentation dominates, and random acts don't create lasting behavior change.

The Solution: Kind30 is a mobile-first platform challenging users to complete 30 documented acts of kindness in 30 consecutive days. Complete the challenge, receive a "Certified Kind" bracelet with unique serial number, join a global movement
