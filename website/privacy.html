2.1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how 30ActsofKindness NFP collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use 30 Acts of Kindness™.

2.2 Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Password or login credentials
  • State, city, timezone, and profile details
  • Challenge entries, titles, stories, comments, and feedback
  • Uploaded photos, videos, and other content
  • Mailing information for bracelets or recognition items, if applicable

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and language
  • IP address, device identifiers, push tokens, and app activity logs
  • Usage analytics, crash data, and session data
  • Reminder settings and notification interactions

2.3 How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Operate the 30-day challenge
  • Calculate daily activity based on timezone
  • Send reminders and service notifications
  • Moderate content and enforce safety rules
  • Support rewards such as bracelets
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user support
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

2.3.1 Text Messaging (SMS)

We use text messages (SMS) in two ways. First, for account verification: when you sign in, we send a one-time code to confirm your mobile number. Second, for optional reminders: if you turn on Daily Reminder in the app, we send automated reminder texts to help you log your act of kindness — up to two per day, at the times you choose. Reminders require a separate opt-in, and consent is not a condition of using the app.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of reminders at any time by replying STOP to any reminder text, or by turning Daily Reminder off in the app; reply HELP for help. Opting out of reminders does not affect the verification codes needed to sign in.

We do not share or sell your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in status to any third party or affiliate for their marketing purposes. Text messages are delivered through our messaging provider (Twilio) solely to provide these messages on our behalf.

2.4 Legal Bases for Processing

Where GDPR or similar law applies, we may process personal data based on consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligation, or protection of vital interests, where applicable.

2.5 Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with hosting, storage, and infrastructure providers; analytics and crash-reporting tools; moderation and fraud-prevention vendors; email, notification, and customer support providers; payment or donation processors; legal authorities where required by law; and professional advisors.

We do not sell personal information.

2.6 Public Features

Depending on app settings and product design, your display name, city participation, family participation, leaderboard placement, and approved content may be visible to others.

2.7 Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain challenge history, administer contests and bracelets, investigate fraud and safety issues, and comply with legal obligations.

2.8 Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
