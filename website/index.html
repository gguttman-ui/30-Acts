How it works

Kindness, one day at a time

No big commitments. Just a daily nudge and a place to remember what you did.

1

Get today's act

Open the app to a simple, doable act of kindness. Miss a day and the streak restarts — that's what builds the habit.

2

Do it & document

Complete the act, then capture it with a photo, video, or a few words. One-touch logging, then share if you like.

3

Build your streak

Each completed day fills a tile green. Reach day 30 and you've built a habit of kindness that sticks.

Our story

It started with a father and a son.

30 Acts of Kindness began with a simple wish from an 84-year-old father — and his son's decision to build it into something the whole world could join.

Why we exist. Most of us want to be kinder; what we lack is a simple, repeatable way to act on it. One small prompt a day for a month, and a place to remember what you did.

Our organization. 30ActsofKindness NFP is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Illinois (EIN 41-4058016). Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

The bigger picture

One act is a leaf. One person is a tree.

Every act of kindness is a leaf. Every person who takes the 30-day journey grows into a tree. And every community that joins becomes a forest — kindness adding up, one leaf at a time.
