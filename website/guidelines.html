3.1 Our Mission

30 Acts of Kindness™ is a positive, family-friendly community built around kindness, encouragement, service, and respectful participation.

3.2 What We Encourage

We welcome content that:

  • Celebrates kind acts
  • Supports communities and families
  • Shares uplifting stories
  • Motivates healthy participation
  • Encourages generosity, respect, and gratitude

3.3 What Is Not Allowed

Language and behavior:

  • Profanity, obscene or vulgar language
  • Insults, threats, or harassment
  • Hateful or discriminatory language
  • Bullying or intimidation

Sexual content:

  • Pornography or sexually explicit content
  • Sexual nudity or sexually suggestive content
  • Sexual exploitation or solicitation

Violence and danger:

  • Graphic violence or cruelty
  • Violent threats or self-harm encouragement
  • Dangerous or illegal acts framed as challenges

Fraud and dishonesty:

  • Fake submissions or impersonation
  • Spam or scams
  • Leaderboard manipulation or false donation claims

Privacy violations:

  • Posting someone else's private information without permission
  • Uploading content you do not have the right to share

3.4 Compete with Kindness

Family and city competition should remain respectful and friendly. Rivalry is allowed; hostility is not.

3.5 Authentic Participation

Submit only acts you actually completed. Do not upload fake, stolen, manipulated, or misleading proof.

3.6 Reporting

Users may report content or behavior that violates these rules. We may review reports and take action.

3.7 Consequences

Violations may result in:

  • Content removal
  • Temporary restrictions
  • Loss of contest eligibility
  • Suspension or permanent removal
